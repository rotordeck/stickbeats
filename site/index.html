Record your own

Your voice. Your dog. Your radio.

Replace any of the 70 radio sounds with a recording you make in your browser: “arming!”, a bark, a friend shouting “battery!”. No account, no upload.

Audio is recorded and processed on your device. Mix it with any theme or voice pack and download one SD card pack.

Start recording
  1. RecordPick an event, press record and allow the microphone. Up to 30 seconds.
  2. TrimSmart trim suggests the loud part. Adjust start and end, and play it back.
  3. DownloadFill the rest from any theme. The ZIP converts to 16 kHz mono WAV for your radio.
